FINANZNEO YOUTUBE ANIMATED BLACK WORLD — V3

PURPOSE
Every image supports the spoken point visually in the same stylized 3D animated-film world on deep black. The LOOK is fixed. The VISUAL FORM is not fixed.

CORE RULE — MEANING FIRST, FORM FREE
Before choosing a scene, person, object or camera, understand the spoken beat. Then choose the visual form that explains it fastest and looks strongest.

Equal options include:
- isolated-object: one object or detail is enough
- object-detail: a tight crop of the meaningful part
- hands-in-action: only hands when the action matters
- character-moment: a person only when the person adds meaning
- everyday-scene: a real place only when the place adds meaning
- comparison-scene: two sides when contrast is the point
- semantic-object-composition: several objects arranged so their relationship explains the point
- visual-metaphor: a simple visual metaphor when it is instantly understandable
- creative-idea: any clear visual idea that fits the beat
- hybrid-scene-plate: Flow image plus precise Remotion overlay when useful

There is NO scene-first rule, NO people quota, NO object quota and NO location requirement.

APPROVED LOOK — ALWAYS
- stylized 3D animated-feature-film rendering, never photorealistic, never stock photo
- when a person appears: appealing stylized adult character with expressive face and clear body language
- recognizable objects with believable proportions and clear materials
- semi-realistic material cues rendered soft, clean and visibly stylized
- soft natural lighting: warm key, gentle rim, soft contact shadows
- deep seamless black world
- emerald green = positive/solution/saving; warm red-orange = cost/warning/loss; gold = small money/value accent; warm ivory and soft gray = neutral

NATURAL — NOT AI-LOOKING
Every image feels intentional, calm and designed, never like generic AI decoration.
- natural proportions and readable silhouettes
- no decorative clutter
- no random object piles
- no arbitrary S-curves, spirals or floating trails just to make the frame look interesting
- if objects float or sit in empty space, their position must explain a relationship: before/after, cause/effect, choice, accumulation, comparison or hierarchy
- every visible object must answer: why is it here?
- text only exactly the requested words; no invented labels
- no plastic-shiny, over-sharpened or oversaturated look

AI SLOP — FORBIDDEN
- glowing/neon edges
- glass or crystal finance bars/blocks/arrows
- value blocks or value stacks as generic symbols
- random falling arrows or finance icons
- dramatic red glow, smoke, sparks, lens flares, heavy fog
- epic poster drama
- banknote piles used only as decoration
- generic finance collage
- cluttered background

VISUAL RELATIONSHIPS — ALLOWED WHEN THEY MEAN SOMETHING
Objects do not need a table, room or realistic support surface. They may be isolated or arranged in space when the arrangement itself communicates the idea.

Good examples:
- broken headphones -> replacement headphones -> two receipts = buying twice
- one bill beside three recurring monthly bills = repetition
- wallet on one side and terminal on the other = comparison of payment modes
- cracked phone large in frame with repair quote beside it = sudden repair cost

Bad examples:
- thirty coffee cups in a decorative spiral with no additional meaning
- random receipts flying through space only because it looks dynamic
- objects placed on a table just because every image needs a surface

STORY MOMENTS — OPTIONAL
Only when the beat is about something happening, show the decisive second. Otherwise an object, detail, comparison or composition may be stronger.

VARIETY
Do not repeat the previous successful formula automatically. Variation can come from visual form, scale, crop, camera, object relationship, presence/absence of people, or use of space. Same world means same render DNA, not same scene grammar.

GERMAN LABELS
Short German labels are allowed only when they remove ambiguity and belong to the exact object. No normal scene headline, subtitle, CTA or paragraph inside Flow images.

YOUTUBE COMPOSITION
Horizontal 16:9. The main information must be large and readable on a phone. Camera and spatial arrangement are completely content-dependent: close-up, top-down, frontal, side, macro, isolated object, wide scene or controlled spatial composition are all allowed. No default eye-level rule. No forced horizon. No forced foreground/background depth.

PRECISION
Charts with axes, exact mathematical diagrams, tables, checklists, UI and exact data layouts belong to Remotion/SVG/React. Flow may still show objects or a conceptual visual around the idea, but it must not fake precision.

FORBIDDEN
- photorealism / stock-photo look
- old green-gold symbol world as the main idea: bank buildings, shields, vaults, coin mountains, glowing icons
- meaningless abstract finance sculptures: debt clamps, interest magnets, payment tokens, money ribbons, value blocks
- AI-poster look: glowing/glass bars, neon, smoke, sparks, lens flares, epic drama
- dark green-black monochrome frames
- generated dashboards, settings UI, exact charts, tables, checklists or progress bars
- quotes or long text inside generated images
- floating UI cards, tiles or panels; flowcharts
- bright white/colored studio backgrounds
- tiny unreadable diorama framing
- childish toy/plastic/clay look
- real identifiable people or pasted logos
- decorative objects with no explanatory role

PROMPT — ENGLISH, SHORT, CONTENT-DRIVEN
Use this flexible shell:
Stylized 3D animated feature film still, 16:9. [BEST VISUAL IDEA FOR THIS SPOKEN BEAT — OBJECT, DETAIL, ACTION, SCENE, COMPARISON, SEMANTIC OBJECT COMPOSITION OR VISUAL METAPHOR]. [OPTIONAL LOCATION ONLY IF THE LOCATION ADDS MEANING]. Only text: "[max two short German labels]" (or: No text.). Soft natural light, deep black background. Not photorealistic, no logos.

The optional location sentence may be omitted completely. Do not invent a room, table, counter or person to fill the prompt.

MEANING TEST — BEFORE GENERATION
1. What is the single thought the viewer should understand?
2. What visual form explains that thought fastest?
3. Does every object and its position have a reason?
4. Would removing the person/place/table make the image stronger? If yes, remove it.
5. Is the idea clear in about two seconds without voiceover?

QA — REJECT AND REGENERATE THE SAME NUMBER WHEN
- the spoken point is not visually clear
- the composition is decorative instead of explanatory
- a table, room, person or story was added without adding meaning
- floating objects have no meaningful relationship
- it repeats the previous image formula without reason
- it looks like AI slop: glowing, glassy, dramatic, overloaded or generic
- it becomes photorealistic
- it falls back to the old finance-symbol world
- the main subject/information is too small
- text is long, wrong or invented
