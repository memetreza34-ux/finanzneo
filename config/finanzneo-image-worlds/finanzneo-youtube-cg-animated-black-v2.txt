FINANZNEO YOUTUBE CG ANIMATED BLACK WORLD — V2

PURPOSE
The FinanzNeo YouTube image world. Every source image is a frame from a fully CG 3D
animated feature film, showing ordinary German everyday situations on a deep black world.
Real in identity, animated in rendering.

Replaces finanzneo-youtube-grounded-3d-black-v1, which allowed results that drifted into
photography, clay and toy renders.

BASE LOCKS
- SOURCE_VISUAL_LANGUAGE: finanzneo-stylized-3d-animated-black-v9
- FINANZNEO_WORLD_ID: finanzneo-connected-studio-v3
- FINANZNEO_SERIES_LOCK: finanzneo-same-world-v1
- GENERATED_IMAGE_ASPECT_RATIO: 16:9
- TARGET_USE: YouTube Longform image and hybrid visual source

THE CORE SENTENCE
Real in identity, animated in rendering.

- Identity is real: the object is exactly the thing a German household owns, with the
  features it is recognized by.
- Material and light are CG: modeled, sculpted, lit as an animation-film asset.
- Nothing in the frame was ever photographed.

Never write the words Pixar, cartoon, clay or toy into a prompt. Image models read them
as plasticine figures and bath toys. Write out the craft instead.

RENDERING
Frame from a fully CG 3D animated feature film. Everything is modeled and rendered.
No photo texture, no scanned material, no camera grain, no lens dirt, no photography.
Clean sculpted CG materials: soft idealized paint, matte plastic, smooth paper, gently
polished metal.
Shapes are appealing and slightly simplified while staying completely recognizable.
Warm cinematic key light with a soft rim light and gentle bounce. Soft contact shadows.

IDENTITY RULES
An object carries the features by which it is recognized in real life.
Money is recognizable euro banknotes: correct colour for the value, correct proportions,
readable denomination. Never generic coloured paper and never dollars.
Money keeps its own real colour even when that colour is not its semantic role colour.
Invoices, account statements, payslips, rent letters and contracts follow ordinary German
layout, with a readable amount where the scene needs one.
Cars, appliances, packaging and food are ordinary European everyday items.

BACKGROUND
Pure deep black. The objects float in black with soft contact shadows.
A small purposeful fragment of a real place is allowed when the situation needs it — one
shelf section, one doorway, one corner, one laundry nook — and it must fade into the
black within a short distance.
Never a full room, never a generic studio floor, never a desk or table surface, never a
wooden surface, never a horizon, never a gradient backdrop, never coloured background
zones, never particle or glow decor.

PEOPLE
A stylized anonymous 3D animation-film person may appear when the situation is a human
one — shopping, deciding, carrying, waiting, working.
The face is always clearly visible, with stylized eyes, nose and mouth, in front or
natural three-quarter view.
One person, natural simple pose, actually doing something.
Never a real or identifiable person, never photorealistic skin, never a faceless
mannequin, never a blank smooth face, never a hidden face, never a pure back view,
never a crowd.
The situation must still be readable if the person were removed. The person supports the
explanation, it does not replace it.

AT LEAST ONE REAL 3D OBJECT
Every image needs at least one tangible three-dimensional object.
Never build a scene out of paper and documents alone. Paper is what an image model renders
photographically by default, and that is where this world breaks.

ONE RELATION PER IMAGE
An image shows exactly one relation: big/small, near/far, open/closed, tipping/standing,
pressing/being pressed, fits/does not fit.
Plus one telling detail that shows that relation — an overflowing stack, a bolt still
rolling away, a puddle still spreading, a drawer left open, a basket still empty.
No chain of four things. Abstract sentences become simple once a single relation is chosen.

LITERAL FIRST, CREATIVE SECOND
Start from the concrete real situation behind the spoken line. Every prompt carries:
  LITERAL_REAL_WORLD_SITUATION:
  REAL_WORLD_CONTEXT_ANCHOR:
  VOICEOVER_VISUAL_MATCH:
  TRANSFERABILITY_TEST:
  VISUAL_STRATEGY:
  METAPHOR_JUSTIFICATION:
A metaphor is allowed only when it is demonstrably clearer than the real situation, and
must then be justified in METAPHOR_JUSTIFICATION.

NO IMAGE-TO-IMAGE REFERENCE
Never attach, upload or reference another image, not even an approved one from the same
series. Flow has no memory between jobs; an image reference carries subject and layout
across while the rendering style still falls back to the photographic default.
Consistency comes only from the written style lock repeated in every prompt.

COLOUR ROLES
Deep black is the world. Emerald green is reserve, solution and positive value. Warm
red-orange is cost, loss, warning, debt and problem. Warm ivory and soft gray are neutral
real objects. Subtle gold is a money accent only.
Never force a colour onto an object whose meaning does not justify it. No rainbow palette,
no neon cyberpunk, no excessive glow. Money is the exception and keeps its real colour.

TEXT IN THE IMAGE
Only the short German object labels listed in that prompt, small, printed, stamped,
engraved or hung as a small tag on or directly at the object they describe.
A label is never the largest or most prominent element in the frame.
Never build a word, headline, phrase or number as a large sculpted 3D object.
Never invent extra text, form fields, table headers, addresses, brand names or placeholder
copy. Never invent a number that has not been researched.
No headline, no subtitle, no CTA, no explanatory sentence.

COMPOSITION
Horizontal 16:9. One clear main situation. Cause and effect readable in the same frame.
Understandable in one to two seconds without audio.
Hero objects large enough to read on mobile, laptop and TV.
Keep calm black space along the top edge: the hero sits in the middle and lower two
thirds so a subheading can be placed above the image in Remotion. Nothing important
touches the top edge.
Useful negative space, never sterile. Foreground, midground and background depth where it
helps. The still frame must already work before any Remotion motion is added.

VARIETY ACROSS THE SERIES
Do not reuse the same object family scene after scene. Same world, different situation:
an appliance nook, a shelf of purchases, a counter of documents, personal belongings, a
row of calendar sheets receding into depth, a car, bank paperwork, drawers, a supermarket
shelf section.

PROMPT LENGTH AND ORDER
Keep prompts short — roughly fifty lines including the metadata block. Long rule blocks
consume the attention the scene needs.
Order: style block, then SCENE, then a short style reminder at the end.
The style appears at the front AND the end; image models weight the ending strongly.
Write every prompt individually for the exact spoken point. Never a generic master prompt
with a few changed nouns.

DATA AND ABSTRACT BEATS
Some beats are better explained by Remotion than by a generated image: verified numbers,
charts, timelines, comparisons of amounts. Do not force this world onto every beat. Plan
those as animation or data visuals instead.

QUALITY CHECK — REGENERATE THE SAME IMAGE NUMBER WHEN
- the frame looks photographic, or like a photo of real paper on a real desk
- it looks like clay, plasticine, a soap bar or a bath toy
- objects are featureless rounded lumps without real construction
- it became a toy town, toy houses, toy cars or a tabletop diorama
- a word or number was built as a large 3D sculpture
- invented text, fake form fields or an unresearched number appears
- money looks like generic coloured paper, or like dollars
- the scene is only paper and documents
- a person has no clearly visible face, or is shown from behind
- black is no longer the dominant world, or a full room appeared
- more than one relation is being shown and the point is no longer readable
- a vault, safe, piggy bank, coin stack, shield or upward arrow replaced the real situation
- the top edge is crowded and leaves no room for a subheading

GOOGLE FLOW EXECUTION
Generate exactly one image at a time. Wait for completion. Rename immediately. Run the
quality check. On failure regenerate the same image number. Only then continue.
Never batch, never queue, never generate several images in parallel.
