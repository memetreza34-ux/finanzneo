EXPERIMENTAL V10 VISUAL WORLD — MANDATORY
==================================================

MATURE EDITORIAL 3D ILLUSTRATION
- clearly stylized 3D, never photorealistic
- should feel like a premium editorial magazine illustration translated into 3D
- graphic, designed shape language with simplified planar forms and restrained bevels
- strong silhouettes; large readable forms before tiny detail
- mature, calm, precise visual language
- one coherent abstraction level across people, hands, objects and environments

ABSOLUTELY AVOID
- Pixar-like / Disney-like / DreamWorks-like character design
- generic animated-movie protagonist look
- cute oversized heads or big eyes
- toy, clay, vinyl figure or collectible figurine look
- glossy AI-plastic skin or objects
- mobile-game / AAA-game cinematic render
- photorealistic stock-photo look
- product-advertising render
- fuzzy black circular vignette around a tiny scene
- miniature island floating in darkness
- heavy bokeh, bloom, neon rim light, lens flare

ADULT CHARACTER LANGUAGE
- believable adult body proportions, roughly 1:7 to 1:7.5 head-to-body ratio
- normal-sized head, hands and feet
- simplified adult face with small eyes, restrained nose and mouth, clearer jaw and cheek planes
- minimal skin detail; matte skin shading
- expression primarily through posture, eyebrow angle, head position and subtle mouth shape
- hair designed as several clean masses, not individual photoreal strands
- broad matte clothing forms with a few meaningful folds

OBJECT LANGUAGE
- keep real functional construction recognizable
- simplify into large planes
- restrained edges and bevels, not fully rounded toy geometry
- matte or satin materials; restrained highlights
- no glossy product-shot finish

ENVIRONMENT LANGUAGE
- believable architectural fragments, not a fully cluttered room
- use a real floor, counter, table or wall plane so the scene feels grounded
- deep black is clean negative space around the architecture
- let architecture crop/open naturally into black
- NEVER use a fuzzy vignette halo as the transition to black

CAMERA
- editorial and graphic, not movie-still cinematic
- natural 45–70 mm perspective for human/environment scenes
- restrained depth of field; important planes stay readable
- no dramatic ultra-wide distortion unless specifically required

LIGHTING
- one broad soft directional key
- restrained fill
- subtle separation only when required
- broad readable shadows
- no neon rim glow, no fog, no bloom

COLOR
- graphite / warm gray / ivory = neutral
- emerald = positive / protected / available
- warm red-orange = cost / pressure / warning
- muted brushed gold = money/value only
- accent colors stay limited; neutral world remains dominant

TEXT
- only exact short labels requested by the scene
- no posters, wall writing, decorative typography, headlines, subtitles, logos or random text
