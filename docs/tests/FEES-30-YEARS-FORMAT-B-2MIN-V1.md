Animations are not dispatched from the Finance Motion Library. Each one lives in its exact scene folder and is authored for that scene's spoken point.

Illustrative calculation: 10,000 EUR start + 300 EUR monthly over 30 years, monthly compounding, 7% p.a. vs 6% p.a. ≈ 447,156 EUR vs 361,580 EUR; rounded in the reel to 447,000 EUR vs 362,000 EUR and ≈85,600 EUR difference. This is an explanatory example, not a forecast.
