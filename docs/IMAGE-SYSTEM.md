# FinanzNeo — verbindliches Bildsystem

Dieses Dokument definiert den aktuellen Bildstil für alle FinanzNeo-Reels.

## Ziel

Jedes Bild muss einen gesprochenen Satz visuell erklären. Es soll keine Dekoration und kein überfülltes Poster sein.

Standardlogik:

`Ausgangspunkt → Entwicklung → Ergebnis`

## Fester Stil

- premium isometric editorial finance illustration
- dark charcoal background
- deep green accent color
- restrained gold details for money
- clean modern 3D illustration
- premium matte materials
- subtle glass details
- soft volumetric lighting
- strong but clean depth
- not photorealistic
- not cartoonish
- not childish

## Safe Areas

Bei jedem vertikalen Bild:

- obere 18 % frei und ruhig
- untere 22 % frei und ruhig
- keine wichtigen Objekte, Pfeile, Labels oder Charts in diesen Bereichen
- Hauptszene im mittleren Bereich

Remotion platziert später:

- Überschrift oben
- Untertitel unten
- bei Bedarf Zahlen, Quellen, Diagramme und CTA

## Textregeln

Standard: kein Text im Bild.

Erlaubt sind höchstens drei kleine deutsche Labels:

- maximal ein bis drei Wörter
- nur direkt neben dem passenden Objekt
- nur im mittleren Bildbereich
- keine Überschrift
- kein Untertitel
- keine Absätze
- keine ganzen Sätze
- keine englischen Titel

## Kompositionsregeln

- ein zusammenhängendes Einzelbild ist der Standard
- 2 bis 4 große Hauptelemente
- wenige unterstützende Details
- starke Größenhierarchie
- klare Verbindung durch Weg, Pfeil, Größenänderung oder Kontrast
- auf dem Smartphone sofort verständlich
- keine vielen kleinen Panels
- keine zufälligen Mini-Icons
- keine leeren Einzelobjekte
- keine Produktwerbung

## Markenregel

Reale Marken, Logos und Oberflächen nur dann verwenden, wenn das Thema genau diese Marke behandelt. Ansonsten generische originale Finanzobjekte aus derselben FinanzNeo-Welt verwenden.

## Standardprompt

```text
Create a premium isometric editorial finance illustration for a German faceless finance explainer video.

VISUAL STYLE:
Dark charcoal background, deep green accent color, restrained gold details for money, clean modern isometric 3D illustration, soft volumetric lighting, premium matte materials, subtle glass details, strong depth, polished editorial design, not photorealistic, not cartoonish, not childish.

IMPORTANT COMPOSITION:
Create one complete explanatory scene with multiple connected elements instead of a single isolated object.

The image must communicate one clear financial idea through visual storytelling:
starting point → visible development → understandable result.

Use only 2–4 large primary objects and a small number of supporting elements. The scene must remain easy to understand on a smartphone screen.

SAFE AREAS FOR REMOTION:
Keep the upper 18 percent of the image clean and visually quiet.
Keep the lower 22 percent of the image clean and visually quiet.
Do not place important objects, charts, arrows, labels or decorative elements inside these areas.
The main explanatory scene must be positioned in the central area of the vertical frame.

TEXT RULES:
Do not add a headline, subtitle, paragraph, explanatory sentence or large text card.
Remotion will add the headline and subtitles later.

Use no text by default.
Only when absolutely necessary, include a maximum of three very small German labels, each no longer than one to three words.
Place labels directly beside the object they describe and only inside the central illustration area.

VISUAL CLARITY:
Use clear spatial separation, strong scale hierarchy and connected visual movement.
The viewer should understand the financial concept instantly without reading a paragraph.
Avoid clutter, excessive icons, many tiny panels, complicated dashboards and decorative filler.

BRAND RULES:
Do not use real brands, company logos or copyrighted interfaces unless the specific topic requires them.
Otherwise use original generic financial objects belonging to one consistent FinanzNeo illustration world.

The result must feel like one frame from a consistent premium finance explainer series, not an advertisement, product render, presentation slide or crowded infographic poster.

Vertical 9:16 composition.
```

## Beispiel: Inflation

Satz:

> Inflation sorgt dafür, dass dein Geld jedes Jahr an Kaufkraft verliert.

```text
Create a premium isometric editorial finance illustration explaining the sentence:

“Inflation sorgt dafür, dass dein Geld jedes Jahr an Kaufkraft verliert.”

VISUAL STYLE:
Dark charcoal background, deep green accent color, restrained gold details for money, clean modern isometric 3D illustration, soft volumetric lighting, premium matte materials, subtle glass details, strong cinematic depth, not photorealistic, not cartoonish.

CREATE ONE CONNECTED EXPLANATORY SCENE:

In the lower-left central area, show a premium dark wallet containing several clearly recognizable euro banknotes.

A glowing green path moves diagonally upward through the scene toward two shopping baskets.

Along the path, several euro banknotes gradually become smaller, thinner and slightly more transparent, visually representing the continuous loss of purchasing power.

The first shopping basket contains a normal amount of generic groceries.

The second shopping basket, positioned farther along the path, contains noticeably fewer groceries even though the same amount of money was used.

Near the second basket, include a small clean upward-trending price chart to represent rising prices.

The complete visual story must communicate:
same money → rising prices → fewer products.

COMPOSITION AND SAFE AREAS:
Keep the upper 18 percent completely clean and dark for a Remotion headline.
Keep the lower 22 percent completely clean and dark for Remotion subtitles.
Place all important objects, arrows and charts inside the central area.
Use a spacious diagonal composition with clear separation between the wallet, shrinking banknotes and shopping baskets.

TEXT:
Do not add a headline, subtitle, paragraph or explanatory sentence.

Optional small German labels only:
“Kaufkraft”
“Preise steigen”
“Inflation”

Use a maximum of three labels.
Keep them small, subtle and close to the corresponding object.
Do not place any label in the upper or lower safe area.

Do not use real brands, supermarket logos, product packaging or company names.
Use only original generic objects from the established FinanzNeo finance illustration world.

The viewer must instantly understand that the same amount of money buys fewer goods over time.

Vertical 9:16 composition.
```

## Beispiel: Notgroschen

Satz:

> Ein Notgroschen verhindert, dass eine unerwartete Rechnung dich direkt in Schulden bringt.

```text
Create a premium isometric editorial finance illustration explaining the sentence:

“Ein Notgroschen verhindert, dass eine unerwartete Rechnung dich direkt in Schulden bringt.”

VISUAL STYLE:
Dark charcoal background, deep green accent color, restrained gold details for money, clean modern isometric 3D illustration, soft volumetric lighting, premium matte materials, subtle glass details, polished editorial design, not photorealistic, not cartoonish, not childish.

CREATE ONE CONNECTED EXPLANATORY SCENE:

On the left side of the central composition, show a broken washing machine beside an unexpectedly high repair invoice.

In the center, show a transparent emergency-fund container filled with euro banknotes and coins. A subtle glowing green shield surrounds the container, representing financial protection.

A clear green path leads from the emergency fund toward the repair invoice, showing that the saved money covers the unexpected cost.

On the right side, show a red credit-card debt symbol or a growing stack of unpaid bills being blocked by the green shield.

The complete visual story must communicate:
unexpected expense → emergency fund pays → debt is avoided.

COMPOSITION AND SAFE AREAS:
Keep the upper 18 percent completely clean and visually quiet for a Remotion headline.
Keep the lower 22 percent completely clean and visually quiet for Remotion subtitles.
Do not place important objects, labels, arrows, bills or decorative elements inside these safe areas.
Position the complete explanatory scene inside the central area of the vertical frame.

VISUAL CLARITY:
Use only four main visual elements:
1. broken washing machine
2. repair invoice
3. protected emergency fund
4. blocked debt symbol

Use strong size hierarchy, clear spatial separation and one obvious visual path.
The concept must be understandable instantly on a smartphone screen.

TEXT RULES:
Do not add a headline, subtitle, paragraph or explanatory sentence.

Use no text by default.
Only when necessary, include a maximum of three small German labels:
“Notgroschen”
“Rechnung”
“Schulden”

Keep every label subtle, small and directly beside the corresponding object.
Do not place labels in the upper or lower safe areas.

Do not use real banks, credit-card brands, appliance companies, logos or copyrighted interfaces.
Use only original generic objects from the established FinanzNeo illustration world.

The image must feel like one frame from a consistent premium finance explainer series, not an advertisement, presentation slide or crowded infographic.

Vertical 9:16 composition.
```

## Ablehnungskriterien

Ein Bild wird neu erstellt, wenn mindestens einer dieser Punkte zutrifft:

- oben oder unten ist die Safe Area belegt
- zu viel Text im Bild
- englische Überschrift ohne Grund
- mehr als drei Labels
- wichtige Objekte sind zu klein
- Szene zeigt nur ein isoliertes Objekt
- Konzept ist ohne Erklärung nicht verständlich
- Bild sieht wie Werbung oder Produkt-Render aus
- zu viele kleine Panels oder Karten
- Stil wirkt kindlich, cartoonhaft oder fotorealistisch
- Bild passt nicht zur FinanzNeo-Farbwelt
