FINANZNEO CONNECTED STUDIO V3 — MONDAY REEL IMAGE WORLD

CORE IDEA:
Create premium fintech editorial 3D illustrations in which the OBJECTS strongly resemble believable real-world objects in shape, construction and material behaviour, while the overall image still clearly looks like a designed 3D editorial explanation rather than a camera photograph or advertising product shot.

REALITY LEVEL — IMPORTANT:
- smartphone, laptop, headphones, paper contracts, envelopes, price tags, coins, magnifying glass and other everyday objects should be immediately recognizable and strongly reality-based
- use believable material detail: glass reflections, paper thickness and folds, plastic seams, brushed metal edges, fabric texture, printed paper tabs, realistic euro-coin relief, subtle wear where natural
- do NOT simplify everything into chunky toy blocks, generic icons or app-like tiles
- do NOT make the scene childish, clay-like or toy-like
- the final image remains a premium 3D editorial illustration, not a real camera photo
- real brands/logos may be used only when explicitly relevant; otherwise use neutral products

VISUAL LANGUAGE:
- ONE dominant financial idea/metaphor per image
- 3–6 supporting objects maximum
- every object must explain a part of the spoken sentence; no random decoration
- group the objects into ONE connected still life with clear foreground, midground and depth
- natural asymmetry, realistic overlap and soft contact shadows
- the image must be understandable in about one second on a phone
- avoid empty minimal product-shot compositions

COLOR LANGUAGE:
- deep charcoal green-black base world
- vivid emerald and mint accents for structure/positive guidance
- cream/off-white paper and neutral product materials where natural
- gold only for euro coins, cash and financial value
- warm red-orange only for risk, debt, hidden cost or warning

BACKGROUND — HARD RULE:
Use ONE single seamless continuous deep charcoal green-black background across the entire image.
The same material, tone and gradient must run from edge to edge.
NO horizontal divisions.
NO top section and bottom section.
NO floor-wall boundary.
NO horizon line.
NO room, desk, shelf, office, shop or visible studio set.
NO panels or layered backdrop.
Use only one subtle continuous gradient/vignette.
Objects may cast soft contact shadows directly onto this uninterrupted background.

TEXT RULE:
- Cover only: exact requested headline + exact requested subline
- Normal scene images: no headline, no subtitle, no sentence, no paragraph, no CTA
- normal scene images may contain ONLY the explicitly requested short German object labels
- labels must be small, clean, correctly spelled and physically attached to or placed directly beside the relevant object
- no invented extra words, dates, numbers or fake contract text

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:
- camera-photo look or lifestyle photography
- luxury commercial product-ad composition
- generic chunky infographic boards as the default hero
- toy-like simplified electronics
- Pixar, clay, childish cartoon look
- UI dashboards, app screens, cards, chips, widgets, HUD
- microchip or circuit-board visual language
- mini isometric dioramas, game boards, satellite orbits
- abstract glowing line networks, finance streams, tubes, rails, roads, tracks
- wealth towers, monoliths, meaningless geometric blocks
- multiple disconnected mini-scenes
- visible room/floor/wall architecture

ASPECT RATIOS FOR THIS REEL:
- Bild 00 cover: vertical 9:16
- Bild 01 / 04 / 07 / 10: strict square 1:1, preferably 1080×1080
