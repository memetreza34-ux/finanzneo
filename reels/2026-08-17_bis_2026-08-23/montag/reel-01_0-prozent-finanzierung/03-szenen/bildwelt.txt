TANGIBLE FINANCE EDITORIAL 3D.

Create a believable premium financial everyday world, not abstract fintech sculpture art.
Use recognizable physical objects: real-looking euro coins and banknote bundles, receipts, contracts, price tags, envelopes, wallets, calendars, product packaging, shopping objects, pens, paper, glass savings containers and subtle desk/environment details.

Visual language:
- sophisticated 3D editorial illustration, adult and premium
- rich spatial depth with foreground, midground and background
- deep charcoal / dark green environment, but NEVER an almost empty black void
- emerald/mint for positive guidance and structure
- gold only for money/value
- warm red-orange for risk, debt, warnings or pressure
- realistic material cues: paper, brushed metal, glass, matte plastic, fabric, wood/stone desk details
- cinematic soft key light + practical ambient lights
- strong large hero motif occupying 85–95% of useful square width

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:
- abstract tubes, pipes, rails or glowing tracks as the main metaphor
- generic wealth towers, empty monoliths, futuristic blocks or meaningless columns
- sci-fi corridors, neon tunnels, game-level environments
- dashboards, app UI, infographic cards as the main image
- almost empty studio-black product shots
- Pixar, clay, toy-like or childish look

ASPECT RATIOS:
- Cover only: vertical 9:16
- Normal scene images: strict square 1:1, preferably 1080×1080

TEXT:
- Cover: exact requested German cover text only
- Normal images: no headline, no subtitle, no explanatory sentence
- only explicitly requested short object labels
