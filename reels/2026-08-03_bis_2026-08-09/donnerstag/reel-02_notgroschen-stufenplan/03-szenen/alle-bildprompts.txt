VERBINDLICHE BILDWELT FÜR JEDEN PROMPT

Premium fintech editorial 3D render style. Background is deep charcoal green-black. Accents in vivid emerald and mint green. Gold only for euro coins, money and financial value. Warm red-orange light only for danger, debt, loss or blocked money. Use smooth rounded 3D geometry, soft bevelled edges, premium matte and glass materials, confident high-contrast studio lighting with a bold emerald rim light.

Each image should use ONE dominant visual metaphor and only a few supporting objects. A stylized anonymous 3D adult person may be included when useful.

PERSON RULE — VERBINDLICH:
Whenever a person appears, the face must be clearly visible with readable stylized eyes, nose and mouth. Prefer a front-facing or natural three-quarter view. No faceless mannequin, no blank face, no hidden face, no full back view and no person turned completely away from camera. The person remains stylized and anonymous, never photorealistic and never a real identifiable human.

TEXT RULE:
No headline. No subtitle. No explanatory sentence. Only 1–3 short German object labels directly near the object they describe. Small to medium clean sans-serif text. Labels must never dominate the composition.

BACKGROUND CONTINUITY — VERBINDLICH:
Use ONE single continuous deep charcoal green-black background from the top edge to the bottom edge.
NO horizontal transition lines. NO darker/lighter top band. NO darker/lighter bottom band. NO separate background panels. NO visible horizon-like strip caused by a background color change.
A subtle natural studio gradient/vignette is allowed only if it remains smooth and seamless across the whole image.

COMPOSITION LOCK:
Vertical 9:16. Keep the top 15 percent completely free of objects, labels and effects, but use the SAME continuous background there. Keep the bottom 25 percent completely free of objects, labels and effects, but use the SAME continuous background there. These are only uncluttered negative-space areas, NOT separate visual zones. Place the complete visual story inside the middle 60 percent.

FORBIDDEN:
No UI dashboards, no app screens, no tiny isometric diorama, no neon tunnel, no sci-fi corridor, no many-room studio architecture, no miniature game level, no clutter, no giant typography, no full sentences, no random labels, no Pixar, no clay, no faceless people, no hidden faces, no horizontal top/bottom background bands.
