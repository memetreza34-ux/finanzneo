FÜR JEDEN PROMPT GILT (an jeden Prompt anhängen):

STYLE (fixed): Soft hand-drawn 2D editorial illustration on a warm cream background, gentle pencil-like
outlines, softly painted flat fills, muted earthy palette (sand, terracotta, sage green, dusty blue, warm
gray) with one stronger accent color for the key information. Never a photo, never glossy 3D.
Use the attached style reference ONLY for line quality, cream background and colors — NOT for how people look.

PEOPLE (new, strict): People are very simple schematic characters, like in a calm explainer graphic:
round head, hair as one flat shape, two small dot eyes, a tiny line mouth, no nose, no wrinkles, no beard
stubble, no realistic facial detail, simple rounded body without folds. Emotions only through eyebrows,
mouth line and body posture. People stay small to medium in the frame; the information is the hero.

COMPOSITION (new): Schema first, scene second. One big clear object or diagram with a short German label
or number, generous empty cream space, at most 3–4 elements. No room interiors, no furniture or
decoration unless it explains the sentence.

TEXT: Only the exact words and numbers written in quotes may appear in the image. Never invent extra text.
