STYLE AUTHORITY
This project uses the approved FinanzNeo grounded stylized-3D black world. Deep black alone is NOT the style. Every Flow image must have tangible premium stylized-3D material presence, physical depth, believable proportions, soft studio light, readable highlights, contact shadows and restrained grounded reflections where useful.

The visual form may be simple, but the rendering world may not change.
- a symbol must look like a tangible premium 3D object, not a flat icon
- a number may dominate the frame, but must be integrated with premium material/light/depth, not a presentation slide
- a chart may be simple and front-facing, but must retain refined V9 material/light treatment, not Excel/PowerPoint styling
- a UI example must be a tangible designed panel/device/object with depth and contact shadow, not a screenshot or flat dashboard card
- labels may be front-facing and simple, but must sit on/with designed physical elements rather than floating generic rectangles

CORE STYLE
- premium clearly stylized 3D animation-film / high-end illustration
- tangible semi-realistic paper/plastic/metal/glass material cues
- deep seamless black dominant world
- refined rounded geometry where appropriate
- real depth and physical thickness
- clean soft studio lighting
- readable contact shadows
- subtle grounded reflections when useful
- emerald for positive/solution
- warm red-orange for cost/risk/debt
- ivory and soft gray neutral
- gold only as a small money/value accent
- never photorealistic
- never generic corporate 3D
- never generic black-background finance graphics

SIMPLE EXPLAINER FORMS
Simple compositions are still encouraged when clearer: symbol/object focus, one number, clean chart, route/goal metaphor, UI example, asset group, concept cluster, short sourced quote/key statement, or classic scene. But all must use the same physical/material FinanzNeo V9 rendering.

HARD FAIL / REGENERATE
Reject even if numbers/text are correct when the image looks like:
- flat 2D infographic
- plain colored rectangles with text
- generic presentation slide
- stock/vector icon pack
- screenshot/dashboard
- Canva/social card
- PowerPoint/Excel chart
- sterile floating boxes with no material depth or grounding

CAMERA
Front-facing or near-front, eye-level, level horizon, natural perspective. Charts and diagrams exactly straight-on with undistorted axes/labels/proportions.

horizontal 16:9 source image. Important subjects large enough for TV, laptop and mobile. One clear idea per image. Short German labels only when useful.
