VERBINDLICHE BILDWELT
Create CLEARLY STYLIZED premium 3D CGI financial editorial explainer images. Every image must look deliberately designed, dimensional and content-specific — not like a realistic office scene, not like a product photo and not like fintech software.

OLD FINANZNEO PROMPT DNA
Every image prompt follows this structure:
MAIN IDEA
SCENE
FULL STYLE LOCK
BACKGROUND / ENVIRONMENT
ALLOWED TEXT
PERSON RULE when relevant
NEGATIVE
QA

MAIN IDEA
Exactly one spoken idea per image. The viewer should understand it in roughly two seconds.

SCENE IDEA RULE
Invent an actual visual mechanism for the idea. Use recognizable 3D objects, figures, environments, actions, scale contrasts or physical metaphors. Do not simply place the topic object on a table.

Good scene logic can include:
- reveal with magnifying glass
- small-vs-large physical contrast
- objects being stacked, split, stamped, clipped, pulled, opened, weighed, transformed or compared
- a stylized person performing a clear action
- multiple recognizable everyday objects arranged as one coherent story
- a scene-specific environment when it helps understanding

3D FORM LANGUAGE
- clearly stylized premium 3D CGI
- chunky substantial volumetric objects
- simplified, slightly exaggerated proportions where useful
- smooth rounded geometry and soft bevels
- visible thickness, depth and material contrast
- premium polymer, metal, ceramic, card-like cream surfaces, glass, fabric, wood or any other fitting material
- strong foreground / midground / background separation
- natural overlap and contact
- strong but soft contact shadows
- mild depth-of-field allowed
- polished editorial CGI finish

COLOR FREEDOM
The FinanzNeo world is not limited to only green, orange, ivory and gold.
Any color may be used when it makes the object, person or environment clearer.
- deep charcoal / green-black remains a recurring base anchor
- emerald/mint can signal positive direction
- warm red-orange can signal cost, debt or warning
- gold/brass can signal money/value
- warm cream works well for information surfaces
- blue, cyan, yellow, orange, red, violet, natural skin tones, clothing colors and environmental colors are all allowed when they fit
Do not recolor every object into the brand palette just for consistency.

CHARACTERS
People are allowed whenever they make the story clearer.
Use stylized adult CGI characters with readable body language. If a face is visible, include eyes, nose and mouth. No real identifiable person and no photorealistic portrait.

TEXT
Short German labels, prices, percentages or short questions are allowed when they are part of the scene and useful for understanding. No long paragraphs, subtitles or CTA sentences. No accidental FinanzNeo logo or watermark.

PRECISION
Dense charts, mathematical curves, tables, long checklists and exact UI states belong to Remotion/SVG/React. Flow can still create the 3D scene or metaphor around them.

BACKGROUND / ENVIRONMENT
Default: premium deep charcoal / green-black FinanzNeo atmosphere.
But a real or stylized environment may fill the frame when useful. Do not force every scene into an empty black studio.

HARD FAIL — SOFORT NEU GENERIEREN
- photorealistic / stock-photo look
- realistic boring desk-and-paper still-life
- flat infographic / PowerPoint / Canva poster
- dashboard / HUD / control panel
- floating card/tile/module grid
- generic finance icon collage
- generic corporate stock 3D
- tiny isometric diorama
- sterile luxury product pedestal
- Pixar / clay / toy look
- huge empty black space with tiny subject
- scene has no visual action, relation, contrast or story
- prompt merely paraphrases the narration instead of inventing a visual idea

QA
PASS only when:
- one idea is understood in about two seconds
- the image unmistakably looks like premium stylized 3D CGI
- the visual idea is specific and content-driven
- objects/figures/colors are chosen because they help the idea
- depth, overlap, contact or action are clearly visible
