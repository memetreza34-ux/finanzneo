CORE
FinanzNeo is a premium stylized animation-film / editorial finance world. The world stays consistent, but the visual form is chosen from the content instead of forcing everything into the same physical 3D template.

MODE A — GROUNDED SCENE
Use for real situations/objects and visible cause/effect.
- real recognizable objects
- believable proportions
- medium-close editorial framing
- natural perspective
- 0–3 useful supporting objects
- clear depth, overlap, material cues and soft contact shadows
- local context may dissolve into the deep black/charcoal-green world
- do not create a sterile product shot or tiny diorama

MODE B — SIMPLE EXPLAINER
Use when a simple visual communicates faster than a literal scene.
Allowed: large number, symbol, simple chart, comparison, target/route/goal metaphor, asset group, concept cluster, short quote/key statement, simple UI/settings explanation.
- front-facing or near-front
- large useful-frame occupancy
- clean hierarchy
- polished 2.5D / stylized editorial treatment is allowed
- keep FinanzNeo colors, lighting and quality
- do NOT turn values/text into arbitrary thick plaques, tiles, cards, panels, blocks or pedestals

CHART / DIAGRAM / DATA
- straight-on front view
- no skewed axes or 3/4 chart plane
- mathematically correct proportions
- no Excel/PowerPoint default style
- no decorative fantasy 3D bars

UI / SETTINGS
- only when the state itself is the point
- clean fictional/unbranded layout
- straight-on and readable
- no screenshot realism
- do not make a thick floating control panel just to appear 3D

COLOR
- emerald = positive / solution / growth
- warm red-orange = risk / cost / debt / warning
- warm ivory / soft gray = neutral
- gold only as small money/value accent
- black/charcoal-green remains dominant

LIGHTING + QUALITY
- premium clean studio light
- readable highlights and shadows
- soft contact shadows where physical objects touch
- adult stylized 3D/editorial quality
- never photorealistic, childish clay/toy/Pixar or generic corporate 3D

GLOBAL HARD FAIL
- main information too small
- mostly empty black space
- floating tile/card/module system
- dashboard/control-panel/HUD without content reason
- arbitrary plaques/blocks for values or text
- sterile product-ad shot
- tiny isometric/diorama framing
- Canva/PowerPoint/Stock-Vector look
- generic finance-icon collage
- gold-luxury AI-finance styling
- visual form more complicated than the spoken point

QUALITY RULE
The image must feel FinanzNeo because of consistent color logic, polished stylized rendering, lighting, hierarchy and explanatory clarity — not because every element was forced into a physical 3D object.
