# Alle Bildprompts zum Kopieren

Hier stehen alle fünf vollständigen Bildprompts direkt hintereinander. Jeden Codeblock einzeln in das Bildmodell kopieren und das Ergebnis unter dem angegebenen Dateinamen speichern.

---

## Bild 1 — Hook

**Dateiname:** `images/scene-01-hook.png`

```text
MESSAGE TO COMMUNICATE:
What happens to 100 euros when someone invests them in an ETF?

SCENE:
A generic stylized 3D investor taps a smartphone while one large glowing 100-euro token enters a clear physical ETF investment path. The path has three immediately recognizable connected stations: a broker gate, a stock-exchange gate and one large ETF share token linked to a transparent diversified fund container. The entire journey is visible as one coherent scene, so the viewer instantly understands that the reel will explain what happens after investing 100 euros in an ETF. Keep the composition simple, bold and easy to read. No company logos and no detailed interface.

GERMAN LABELS:
"100 €", "Broker", "Börse", "ETF-Anteil"

COMPOSITION:
Place the glowing 100-euro token large in the center foreground at the beginning of the connected path. Arrange the broker gate, exchange gate and ETF share from front to back in one clear visual direction. Keep the entire top 15 percent clean for the later hook text and the bottom 25 percent free for subtitles. The 100-euro token and ETF path must already be visible at frame zero.

Create a premium stylized 3D finance illustration for a modern German financial education channel.

The image must feel like a polished editorial fintech explainer illustration: clearly illustrated, never photorealistic, never childish, and never a dashboard.

Use simplified rounded 3D geometry, soft bevelled edges, smooth high-quality materials, subtle reflections, confident studio lighting with bold rim light, gentle ambient occlusion, restrained depth of field and clean cinematic composition. The overall impression should feel bold and vivid rather than muted or timid: strong contrast between the dark background and the glowing accent elements, punchy highlights on the focal point, no washed-out or low-contrast rendering.

Use a deep charcoal to green-black background. Keep objects in recognizable natural colors and use emerald green and mint green as bold, saturated, clearly glowing accent colors for money, growth, positive development, guidance and focal lighting — vivid and confident, never pale or desaturated. Use warm gold, orange or muted red only when the scene needs to represent consumption, cost, risk, warning or loss. Do not apply a global green color cast.

Build one coherent illustrated scene or one clear visual metaphor with one dominant focal point and no more than three to five supporting elements. The scene must feel spatial and narrative, not like loose icons, a UI layout, a presentation slide or a technical diagram.

Stylized 3D human figures may appear when they clarify an action. They must be generic fictional characters with simplified proportions, neutral friendly facial features and modern understated clothing. Never use real people, celebrity likenesses, photorealistic humans, exaggerated animated-movie faces or toy-like clay characters.

Objects and locations must be instantly recognizable but elegantly simplified: wallets, euro coins, apartment buildings, supermarkets, offices, factories, ETF containers, investment trees, shopping carts and similar financial metaphors. Avoid realistic architectural visualization, fantasy machines, overcrowded miniature cities and excessive glowing network lines.

Allow only short, correctly written German labels when they materially improve understanding. Use one to three words per label and no more than three to five labels in total. Do not generate a title, subtitle, paragraph, logo, brand name, watermark or interface text. Main headline, captions and CTA will be added later in Remotion.

Keep the entire top 15 percent of the frame as clean negative space for a later German headline overlay. Keep the entire bottom 25 percent of the frame clear, uncluttered and free of any important subject detail, reserved for a later German subtitle strip — do not place faces, labels or key story elements that low in the frame. Keep the main subject readable on a phone screen. Use a vertical 9:16 composition, premium render quality, balanced visual hierarchy and consistent FinanzNeo art direction.

Outside of the reserved top and bottom zones, the main scene must fill the available space generously and confidently — large, close, dominant in the frame. Do not shrink the subject into a small island surrounded by large areas of empty dark background; empty space is only intentional in the reserved top and bottom zones, not around the sides or center of the main scene.

The image must be one single seamless render filling the entire canvas edge to edge. Never leave a blank, white, gray or differently colored strip, bar or border at the top or bottom of the frame. Never split the background into two visually distinct segments, panels or color bands — the background must be one continuous environment from top to bottom with no visible seam, crop line or letterboxing.

Any pedestal, platform, table or base must sit flush and solid on the ground with zero gap underneath it. Never render a visible slit, crack, glowing line, floating shadow or strip of background showing through between a pedestal/platform and the floor beneath it, and never let a pedestal appear to hover or float above the ground. The base and the ground must read as one uninterrupted solid surface with no visible separation of any kind.

The scene's meaning must be understood immediately and directly, without requiring the viewer to interpret, guess or think about it — someone who already knows the spoken sentence must recognize on the very first glance exactly how this image matches it, with no ambiguity about which object represents what.

Avoid: photorealism, real humans, Pixar style, clay style, flat 2D infographics, UI dashboards, sterile product renders, cyberpunk neon, excessive particles, excessive bloom, floating unrelated icons, overcrowding, long text, logos and watermarks, blank or white margins at the top or bottom edge, letterboxing bars, split or banded backgrounds, visible canvas seams, gaps or slits under pedestals, floating platforms.
```
