# FinanzNeo Image Prompt Template

## Verbindliche Vorlage

```text
MESSAGE TO COMMUNICATE:
[One concise English sentence describing what the viewer must understand.]

SCENE:
[Describe one coherent illustrated scene or one clear visual metaphor. State the main subject first. Add no more than three to five supporting elements. Describe the visible action and financial consequence.]

GERMAN LABELS:
[Optional: list zero to five short German labels, each one to three words.]

COMPOSITION:
[State the focal point, foreground/background relationship, the clean top zone for the headline and the clean bottom ~25% zone reserved for the subtitle strip.]

[Append the full unchanged master block from MASTER-STYLE-PROMPT.md]
```

## Regeln für die Szenenwahl

- Nicht den gesprochenen Satz wortwörtlich illustrieren, sondern seine finanzielle Kernaussage sichtbar machen.
- Eine starke Metapher ist besser als viele kleine Symbole.
- Figuren nur einsetzen, wenn eine Handlung gezeigt werden muss.
- Nie mehrere unabhängige Szenen in ein Bild pressen.
- Das Bild muss auch ohne sichtbaren Text verständlich bleiben.
- Beschriftungen nur verwenden, wenn Verwechslung möglich wäre.
- Das Hauptmotiv und die Farbakzente mutig und kontrastreich halten, nicht blass oder zurückhaltend.
- Die unteren ca. 25 % des Bildes immer frei von wichtigen Bildinhalten halten, weil dort der Untertitel liegt.

## Beispiel 1 — Inflation

**Satz:**

> Inflation nimmt dir nicht direkt Geld vom Konto. Sie sorgt dafür, dass du dir mit demselben Betrag im Laufe der Zeit immer weniger leisten kannst.

```text
MESSAGE TO COMMUNICATE:
The account balance stays unchanged while everyday purchasing power shrinks.

SCENE:
A stylized 3D person stands beside a wallet containing the same fixed amount of euro banknotes. Around the person, a grocery basket, an apartment building and an energy meter gradually become larger and more expensive, while the wallet remains unchanged. The character compares the wallet with the rising everyday costs and looks concerned. Keep the scene coherent, compact and immediately understandable.

GERMAN LABELS:
"Konto", "Preise", "Kaufkraft"

COMPOSITION:
Place the person and wallet as the central focal point. Arrange the three cost symbols around them without creating a dashboard. Keep the top zone clean for the headline and keep the bottom ~25% of the frame clear and uncluttered for the subtitle strip.
```

## Beispiel 2 — Sparrate

**Satz:**

> Langfristig entscheidet nicht nur, wie viel du verdienst, sondern welcher Teil deines Einkommens nach allen Ausgaben regelmäßig investiert wird.

```text
MESSAGE TO COMMUNICATE:
Wealth grows from the portion of income consistently invested after expenses.

SCENE:
A stylized 3D employee receives a stream of euro coins into a wallet. Part of the money flows toward rent, groceries and transport, while one clearly visible remaining stream enters a small investment planter and grows into a healthy coin-bearing tree. The employee actively redirects the remaining coins toward the tree. The scene shows income, necessary expenses and the invested remainder as one connected financial story.

GERMAN LABELS:
"Einkommen", "Ausgaben", "Investieren", "Vermögen"

COMPOSITION:
Keep the employee and wallet near the center, with expenses on one side and the growing investment tree on the other. The tree is the visual payoff. Preserve negative space above the scene and keep the bottom ~25% of the frame clear for the subtitle strip.

```

## Beispiel 3 — ETF-Streuung

**Satz:**

> Mit einem breit gestreuten ETF investierst du nicht nur in eine einzelne Firma, sondern gleichzeitig in viele Unternehmen aus unterschiedlichen Branchen und Ländern.

```text
MESSAGE TO COMMUNICATE:
One diversified ETF distributes a single investment across many different companies and sectors.

SCENE:
A stylized 3D person places one glowing euro coin into a clear ETF container at the center. From the container, several neat branches grow outward into a technology office, a healthcare laboratory, an industrial factory, an energy facility and a consumer-goods shop. The destinations remain part of one balanced illustrated ecosystem rather than separate floating icons.

GERMAN LABELS:
"ETF", "Technologie", "Gesundheit", "Industrie", "Energie"

COMPOSITION:
Make the ETF container the dominant focal point. Arrange the five sectors in a clean semicircle with strong visual hierarchy, open space for the headline above and a clear, uncluttered bottom ~25% of the frame for the subtitle strip.

```

## Beispiel 4 — Notgroschen

**Satz:**

> Ein Notgroschen macht dich nicht reich, aber er verhindert, dass eine unerwartete Rechnung sofort deine langfristigen Investments zerstört.

```text
MESSAGE TO COMMUNICATE:
An emergency fund protects long-term investments from unexpected expenses.

SCENE:
A stylized 3D person stands between a small secure emergency safe and a growing investment tree. A broken washing machine and an urgent car repair bill appear as an incoming problem. The safe opens and covers the unexpected cost, while the investment tree remains untouched and continues to grow. Present this as one clear protective story.

GERMAN LABELS:
"Notgroschen", "Reparatur", "Investment"

COMPOSITION:
Use the safe as the protective foreground element, the person in the center and the intact investment tree as the payoff in the background. Leave uncluttered space above and keep the bottom ~25% of the frame clear for the subtitle strip.

[MASTER STYLE PROMPT]
```
